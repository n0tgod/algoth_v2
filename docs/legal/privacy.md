## What we collect and why

| data | why | where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Apple user identifier and the e-mail Apple shares (may be a private relay address) | your account and sign-in | Sign in with Apple |
| Session tokens (stored hashed), device push token, app build | keeping you signed in; sending notifications about your account’s trades | your device |
| Exchange API key (the secret is encrypted; we keep its first characters for display), its permissions and the account balance read when the key is checked | placing orders for your subscriptions; refusing keys with withdrawal rights; checking that the account holds the deposit | you and the exchange |
| Subscriptions, orders, fills, positions, results and the executor’s log for your account | running the subscription and showing it to you | our server and the exchange |
| Your acceptance of the Terms and Risk Disclosure (version, time, IP address) | proof of what you accepted | our server |
| IP addresses in server logs | security, rate limiting, troubleshooting | your connection |

We do **not** use advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs, and we do not sell or share data for advertising.
