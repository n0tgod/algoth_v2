how it works

Four things that are true about every strategy here

◎

Written by an AI loop

A research pipeline proposes, tests and keeps strategies on its own. Nobody hand-picks a winner to show you: the ranking is a declared rule applied to the whole record.

≋

Three leverage rules

Sigma, Depth and Gated are the same ladder of entries with different sources of leverage, on long, short and two-sided books and on deposits from $100 to $100,000.

▤

A record you can check

Books are recomputed every five minutes, net of fees, slippage and funding. “With backtest” is the whole history under today’s rules; “without” counts only decisions written before their outcome was known.

⏻

You keep control

Trading uses your own exchange key, trading-only and locked to our server’s IP. It starts as a dry run; you flip the switch, and you can stop new entries or close any position from the app or this site.
