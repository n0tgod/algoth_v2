What it is

An AI research loop writes the strategies and keeps a paper book for each: three leverage rules — Sigma, Depth, Gated — on long, short and two-sided books, on deposits from $100 to $100,000.

How the record is kept

Books are recomputed hourly. “With backtest” is the whole history under today’s rules; “without backtest” counts only decisions written before their outcome was known. Where nothing is measured you see a dash, not a zero.

Trading it yourself

With your own Bybit key — trading only, no withdrawals, restricted to our server’s IP — a strategy can trade on your account. It starts as a dry run; you flip the switch, and you can stop new entries or close any position at any time, from the app or from this site.

Honest words

Live results differ from paper. Perpetual futures with leverage can lose the whole deposit. The strategies have not been shown to have a lasting edge; a strategy can stop working, be changed or be closed. Read the Risk Disclosure before you connect a key.
